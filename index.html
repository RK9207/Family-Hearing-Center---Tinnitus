Is tinnitus getting in the way of your day?

Tinnitus feels different for everyone. If any of these sound familiar, an evaluation with an audiologist is a good next step.

Ringing or buzzingIn one or both ears, constant or on and off
Everyday sounds feel too loudSound sensitivity known as hyperacusis
Strong reactions to certain soundsSometimes called misophonia
Trouble following conversationsEspecially in restaurants and other noisy places
Pressure in your ears or dizzinessAlongside ringing or changes in hearing
The TV keeps getting louderOr people seem to mumble when they speak

These symptoms may point to an underlying hearing or balance issue that should be evaluated by an audiologist. Whether you need medical attention, hearing technology, or tinnitus therapy, our team will guide you to the right next step.

Questions about tinnitus care

Do you offer Tinnitus Retraining Therapy (TRT)?

Yes. Dr. Cristina Reddy is certified to administer Tinnitus Retraining Therapy, and Dr. Rachel Frederick also offers it. After your evaluation, your audiologist will talk with you about whether TRT is a good fit.

What happens during my first visit?

We start by understanding your concerns, lifestyle, and goals. Then we complete a comprehensive hearing evaluation, which can include tympanometry, pure-tone audiometry, speech recognition threshold, and word recognition testing.

A standard hearing evaluation typically takes between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on the specific tests required.

Are the hearing tests painful?

No. Hearing tests are completely safe, non-invasive, and painless. During tympanometry you may feel mild pressure, but the test is quick.

Will I get my results the same day?

Yes. Your audiologist will review your results during your appointment and discuss any recommended next steps.

Can you help if everyday sounds feel too loud?

Yes. In addition to tinnitus, our team treats hyperacusis (sensitivity to everyday sounds) and misophonia (strong reactions to specific sounds).

I already wear hearing aids. Can you still help?

Yes. We’re happy to work with patients who already have hearing aids, and regular evaluations help make sure your devices continue to provide optimal benefit.

Do you have a Spanish-speaking audiologist?

Yes. Dr. Cristina Reddy is fluent in English and Spanish and is also capable in American Sign Language.
